# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be useful. Do not be a jerk.

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, and discussions in this repository.

## Standards

Expected:

- Assume competence. The person asking a question has read the code; they are
  stuck, not lazy.
- Critique the code, not the author.
- If you are wrong, say so and move on. It costs nothing and buys a lot.

Not acceptable:

- Personal attacks, or demeaning comments about someone's experience level.
- Harassment of any kind, public or private.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit, remove or reject contributions that violate this, and
may block accounts for repeated violations.

Report problems to the address on the GitHub profile. Reports are handled
privately.
